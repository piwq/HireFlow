### Key Workflow Checkpoints

1.  **Candidate Journey**:
    - Login as `alex_dev@mail.ru`.
    - Browse vacancies in "Вакансии" (Vacancies).
    - Apply to "Senior Python Developer".
    - Go to "ИИ Конструктор" and generate or refine a resume based on profile data.
    - Check "Мои заявки" to see the "Under Review" status.

2.  **HR Kanban & Process**:
    - Login as `hr_expert@hireflow.ru`.
    - Go to "Вакансии" -> "Senior Python Developer".
    - See the "Kanban" board. Move a candidate to "Interview" stage.
    - Go to "Запросы интервью" and approve a slot.

3.  **Manager Feedback**:
    - Login as `tech_lead@hireflow.ru`.
    - Go to "Интервью" to see scheduled calls.
    - After (simulated) interview, go to "Отзывы" and submit evaluation for the candidate.
